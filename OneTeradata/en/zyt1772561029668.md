Chunking is the process of breaking down large documents or data into smaller, manageable, and semantically meaningful pieces called **chunks**.

Select either **Text only** or **Formatted and multi-modal**.

**Text only**: Select if your source content is mostly plain text with predictable structure, such as simple documents, policies, or contracts. This chunking method is quick and splits content primarily by size and configurable rules and may not preserve semantic document structure.

**Formatted and multi-modal**: Select if your source content includes PDFs, rich formatting, tables, or images, where preserving structure improves retrieval quality. This chunking method produces higher quality chunks for semantic search and RAG workloads.

You can select either **Fast** or **High resolution** as extraction strategies.

By default, each chunk contains 512 characters. If required, you can change this chunk size and simultanously select header or footer or both for processing.

**Note**: Selecting header and footer adds the header and footer to the data being processed.
